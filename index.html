Run the engagement layer of your Instagram Business account

Engagent connects to your Instagram professional account through Meta's official API, analyzes each post's performance, sorts every comment by intent — hot leads, buying questions, unhappy customers, fans, business inquiries, spam — and drafts replies grounded in your own business facts. Arabic-first, English built in.

What it does

  • Analyze Now — one tap turns a post's metrics and comments into an actionable insight report.
  • Comment inbox by intent — know which comments make you money and which waste your time.
  • Grounded replies — the agent answers from your Business Brain (location, prices, hours, FAQ), never invented facts.
  • You stay in control — Suggest mode by default, an Approvals queue for sensitive conversations, and a full activity log of every action.
