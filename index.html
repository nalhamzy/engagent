What it does

Three jobs, in the order a shop owner needs them.

Sorts every comment by intent

Hot leads, buying questions, unhappy customers, fans, business inquiries and spam, separated as they arrive, so the comments that are worth money are never under the ones that are not.

  • Replies drafted in your business voice, from the facts you give it: hours, products, prices, FAQs.
  • Reply to a whole group at once, post by post.
  • Hide or delete spam in one pass.

Tracks each lead to won or lost

Every buying signal becomes a lead you can move: new, contacted, won or lost. Add an estimated value, notes and a follow-up date, and get a weekly opportunity report.

  • Overdue follow-ups are flagged.
  • The week at a glance: detected, contacted, won, lost, and the value in play.

Comment to DM

Pick a keyword. When someone comments it, Engagent replies publicly and can also send them a private message. A Pro feature.

A direct-message inbox

The same sorting and drafted replies for DMs, with an optional auto-reply that answers from your Business Brain. It answers people who messaged you first and never sends cold messages.

Jasmine, your assistant

Talk or type to ask what needs you today, and listen to a daily briefing.

English and Arabic

The whole app, and the replies it drafts, work in both.

You stay in control

An assistant that replies under your name has to be predictable. These are fixed rules, not settings you have to find.

You choose how much runs alone

  • Manual: it drafts, you send.
  • Assisted: it sends replies to safe comments, such as buying questions and fans, and asks you first about anything sensitive.
  • Autopilot: it also answers business inquiries, inside the limits you set.

What it will never do on its own

  • Answer a hot lead or an unhappy customer automatically. The one exception is a keyword rule or DM auto-reply that you set up yourself: it sends what you wrote or what your Business Brain says, so use it only for questions that are safe to answer without you.
  • Send a reply it is not sure about. Those wait for your approval.
  • Send past your daily limit, or reply to a comment that contains one of your escalation keywords. If you turn on business-hours-only, it also holds replies outside 08:00 to 20:00 Gulf Standard Time (Muscat); that window is fixed for now.

The Safety Center in the app shows what Engagent will and will not do, and an activity log records every action it takes.

Questions

Do I need a special Instagram account?

A Business or Creator account. Switching from a personal account is free and takes a minute in Instagram’s settings; the app walks you through it.

Do you get my password?

No. You sign in on Instagram’s own screen and Instagram gives Engagent limited access through its official API. You can remove that access from Instagram at any time.

Will it post or change my account?

No. It can read and reply to comments and messages, and hide or delete comments. On the trial and paid plans, spam that Engagent is confident about is hidden automatically; you can change this in the Brain tab. Starter never hides anything on its own. It cannot publish posts.

What happens to my data?

See the privacy policy. You can delete your account and data from inside the app, or from the data deletion page.
